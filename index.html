Fin-z

Every rupee, named.

A product idea for India's Hisaab segment: people who pay digitally every day but still keep their money records on pen and paper. Fin-z brings in every account through RBI's Account Aggregator framework and turns payments into records people understand, in their own words.

Try it: tap any payment to rename it, split a dinner and send payment links, switch months in Insights, filter market signals, and manage which apps your cards are linked to.
Concept, product design and prototype by Harshita Gahlot
Interactive prototype with sample data. Not a live financial product.
